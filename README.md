# Say It

Push-to-talk dictation for macOS. Hold a key, speak, let go — your words appear wherever your cursor is. Transcription runs on your Mac.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/bobrauck-ops/say-it-releases/releases/latest)** — open the `.dmg` and drag **Say It** into **Applications**.

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple Silicon. Say It is signed and notarized by Apple and updates itself automatically.
