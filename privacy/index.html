Say It Privacy Policy

Applies to Say It for Mac and Say It for Windows (including the Microsoft Store version). Last updated 21 November 2026.

In short: Say It turns your speech into text on your own computer. The developer doesn't collect, receive or store anything you say or type, and Say It has no analytics or tracking. Text leaves your computer only if you turn on the optional AI features, and then it goes directly to Anthropic using your own API key.

What Say It does with your voice

  • Say It records from your microphone only while you hold its dictation or command key.
  • The recording is transcribed on your computer by a speech model that runs locally. Audio is kept in memory only until it has been transcribed and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Data stored on your computer

  • Settings, your personal dictionary, and (unless you turn it off) a history of your last 500 dictations are saved in your user account's application data folder.
  • Diagnostic logs record what the app did, for troubleshooting: a log file on Windows, the system log on Mac. They don't contain what you said.
  • If you add an Anthropic API key, it is stored in your system's secure credential store: the Keychain on Mac, Credential Manager on Windows.
  • None of this is sent to the developer. You can delete it at any time; the user guides explain where each item is kept.

Optional AI features (off by default)

AI Polish and command mode use Claude, an AI service from Anthropic. They only work after you add your own Anthropic API key, and only then:

  • AI Polish sends the transcribed text of each dictation of three or more words, the name of the app you're typing in, and your dictionary words, so Claude can tidy the text.
  • Command mode sends the text you selected and your spoken instruction (as text).
  • This text goes directly from your computer to Anthropic. It is handled under your Anthropic account and Anthropic's privacy policy. Audio is never sent.

Network connections

  • Speech model download: on first use, Say It downloads its speech model (Windows: from GitHub; Mac: from Hugging Face, or Apple's own speech files if you choose Apple's engine). No personal information is sent.
  • Updates: the direct-download versions check GitHub for new versions. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store, under Microsoft's policies.
  • Anthropic: only when you use the optional AI features, as described above.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.

Children

Say It is a general productivity tool. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
